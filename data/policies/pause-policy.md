## 1.1 Pause for up to three months
A subscription may be paused for a whole number of months from one through three using pause_subscription.
No subscription charges are collected during the pause, and the subscription automatically resumes after paused_until.
Explain the requested pause duration and the returned resumption date before describing the outcome as complete.
A pause must be chosen by the customer and must not replace a cancellation they have insisted on.
