## 1.1 When to involve a human
Escalate when a tool returns needs_human true, an eligible refund exceeds 20.00 USD, or the required operation is outside the available tools.
Persistent technical issues, unresolved verification, and suspected unauthorized access also require human assistance.
Include a concise issue summary, checks already performed, and a suggested_action with a rationale and policy_ref when a concrete recommendation is supported.
Creating a ticket completes the handoff, not the underlying refund or repair, and the customer must be told which work remains pending.

## 1.2 Ticket priorities
Use normal priority for routine unresolved questions, verification help, and persistent issues without evidence of immediate harm.
Use high priority for a large refund requiring review or a customer who remains unable to access the account after basic checks.
Use urgent priority for credible signs of ongoing unauthorized account activity and low priority for non-blocking feedback or feature requests.
Choose the priority from the observed impact and available evidence rather than the customer's tone alone.
