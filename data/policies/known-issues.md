## 1.1 Offline downloads unavailable
For the fictional demo service, missing offline downloads can be investigated by checking the signed-in account, available device storage, and whether the requested content was downloaded before going offline.
Ask the customer to reconnect and retry a small download while online, then test offline playback again.
Do not recommend deleting downloads as the first step or guarantee that these checks resolve every failure.
If the problem persists, record the device and symptoms in a human-support ticket.

## 1.2 CarPlay playback stops
For the fictional demo service, CarPlay playback interruptions can be investigated by checking whether playback works directly on the phone and whether the phone-to-car connection is stable.
Ask the customer to reconnect and retry playback only while the vehicle is parked.
If phone playback succeeds but CarPlay still fails, collect the phone and connection details and escalate the integration issue.
Do not claim that a service-wide outage or a confirmed software defect exists without evidence.

## 1.3 Playlists appear to be missing
For the fictional demo service, apparently missing playlists may result from signing in to a different account or filtering the library view.
Ask the customer to confirm the account and remove library filters before checking again.
Do not promise playlist restoration or invent a recovery tool that is not available.
If playlists are still missing, create a ticket with the affected playlist names and the customer's description of when they disappeared.

## 1.4 Login and account access
For the fictional demo service, start a login investigation by asking for the visible error and whether the customer is using the intended sign-in method.
Suggest retrying the normal sign-in flow after checking the connection, without asking for passwords or one-time codes.
Escalate persistent login errors or suspected unauthorized access because the available tools cannot reset credentials or establish account ownership.
Do not claim an account-access issue is resolved until the customer confirms they can sign in.
